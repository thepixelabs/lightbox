# Lightbox Contributor License Agreement

## Why this exists

Lightbox is free software under the AGPL, and it is also offered to companies
under a paid commercial licence. That second licence is what funds the work.

PixeLabs can only offer it because PixeLabs holds the rights to all of the
code. If a contribution arrived under the AGPL alone, PixeLabs would have no
right to include it in a commercial licence, and every commercial licence sold
afterwards would cover code it had no right to sell. There is no way to repair
that later without finding every contributor or deleting their work.

So this agreement asks you for one thing: permission for PixeLabs to licence
your contribution on other terms as well. You keep your copyright. You keep the
right to use your own contribution however you like, including in other
projects. Nothing here takes anything away from you.

If you would rather not sign, that is a legitimate choice and no hard feelings.
Open an issue describing the change instead, and it can be implemented
independently.

## How to sign

Comment on your pull request with exactly this line, filling in your details:

```
I have read the CLA document and I hereby sign the CLA.
```

Signatures are recorded against your GitHub account. You sign once, and it
covers every contribution you make afterwards.

If you are contributing as part of your job, please confirm that your employer
permits it, or have someone authorised to bind your employer sign on its
behalf. Many employment contracts assign the copyright in work-related code to
the employer, which means only the employer can grant these rights.
