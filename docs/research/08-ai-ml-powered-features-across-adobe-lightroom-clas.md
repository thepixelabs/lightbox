# AI/ML-powered features across Adobe Lightroom (Classic and cloud)

Adobe has repositioned Lightroom around machine learning over the last five years, and its AI features now split into three tiers by where the compute runs. Fully on-device models power the workhorse tools: AI Denoise (a CNN that denoises raw mosaic data before demosaicing, now accelerated on the Apple Neural Engine), the Enhance pipeline (Raw Details ML demosaicing and 2x Super Resolution upscaling), the masking model family (Select Subject, Sky, Background, Objects, and a People model that decomposes each person into face skin, body skin, hair, eyes, teeth, lips, clothes), Lens Blur (a monocular depth map drives synthetic bokeh with selectable aperture styles and refocusable focus range), and as of the June 2026 release an offline generative model behind the Remove tool. Cloud-side Firefly services handle the heavier generative work (Generative Remove, Reflection Removal) plus the Sensei services that only exist in the cloud-based Lightroom: semantic search without manual keywords, automatic content tagging, face clustering into a People view, Best Photos album highlights, and Assisted Culling that scores focus, eyes-open state, and per-face quality across a shoot. A third tier layers ML outputs into the classic editing model: Adaptive Presets bake AI masks into one-click looks (teeth whitening, sky enhancement, landscape components), Adaptive Profiles do content-aware tone mapping, and Sensei-trained Auto sets the basic sliders per image.

For an open-source clone, nearly every on-device feature has a credible open building block: SAM/SAM2 and BiRefNet-class matting models cover the masking family, Depth Anything V2 covers Lens Blur's depth estimation, Real-ESRGAN/SwinIR cover Super Resolution, LaMa covers non-generative object removal, OpenCLIP/SigLIP plus a local vector index cover semantic search (Immich has already proven this pattern works on consumer hardware), RAM or zero-shot CLIP covers auto-tagging, and ArcFace-style embeddings with HDBSCAN cover people clustering. All of these run acceptably on a mid-range consumer GPU or Apple Silicon via ONNX Runtime with CoreML/DirectML/CUDA execution providers; the pragmatic architecture is a single ONNX-based model-serving layer with per-platform acceleration, mirroring what Adobe itself does. The genuinely hard gaps are the raw-domain models — Adobe's Denoise and Raw Details operate on Bayer/X-Trans mosaic data before demosaic, where open pretrained weights are scarce and per-sensor generalization is the real engineering cost — and diffusion-quality generative fill, which is feasible locally (Stable Diffusion inpainting) but heavy and quality-inconsistent compared to Firefly. Reflection Removal remains research-grade in the open ecosystem.

Priority-wise, a v1 desktop clone gets the most leverage from the masking model family (it underpins local adjustments, adaptive presets, and much of Lightroom's perceived magic), Auto settings, AI Denoise, and content-aware removal; search, tagging, and face clustering are the highest-value library-side features and are also the most commoditized. Generative fill, depth-based Lens Blur, culling, and provenance (C2PA Content Credentials, for which Adobe's own open-source c2pa-rs exists) can follow. A deliberate non-goal for v1 is replicating Adobe's cloud service tier (Firefly servers, generative credits, cross-device Sensei indexing) — the clone's differentiator should be that everything runs locally.

## Feature inventory

| Feature | Priority | Complexity | Description | Building blocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Denoise (raw-domain)** | must | high | Neural denoiser aimed at high-ISO shots that operates on raw mosaic data and outputs a denoised DNG, with a single strength slider and preview; Adobe accelerates it on GPU/Neural Engine and recent releases cut per-photo time sharply on Apple Silicon. | NAFNet, Restormer, SCUNet, DRUNet (KAIR), ELD/PMN low-light raw denoising research code, LibRaw for raw decode, ONNX Runtime (CoreML/DirectML/CUDA EPs), ncnn |
| **Select Subject masking** | must | medium | One-click model that segments the main subject for local adjustments, with recent model refreshes improving edge handling on hard cases like bicycle wheels and busy backgrounds. Feasible locally: salient-object and matting models run in about a second on consumer GPUs. | Segment Anything (SAM/SAM2), BiRefNet, InSPyReNet, U2-Net, ViTMatte for edge refinement, rembg, ONNX Runtime |
| **Select Sky masking** | must | medium | Automatic sky segmentation used both as a standalone local-adjustment mask and inside adaptive sky presets. Straightforward with a semantic segmentation model fine-tuned on a sky class. | SegFormer/Mask2Former trained on ADE20K, U2-Net sky variants, MMSegmentation toolchain, ONNX Runtime |
| **Select Background (subject inversion)** | must | low | Generates a background mask as the complement of the subject mask so users can darken or blur surroundings in one click. Nearly free once subject selection exists. | Same models as Select Subject; mask inversion plus edge feathering |
| **Select People with body-part decomposition** | should | high | Detects each person in the frame and offers per-person sub-masks: face skin, body skin, eyebrows, eyes (iris/sclera), lips, teeth, hair, facial hair, and clothes, enabling targeted retouching. This is a face-parsing plus human-parsing pipeline rather than one model. | BiSeNet face-parsing (CelebAMask-HQ weights), SCHP/LIP human parsing, MediaPipe Face Mesh, RetinaFace/SCRFD detection, SAM2 for cleanup |
| **Select Objects (prompted masking)** | should | medium | User brushes roughly over or drags a box around any object and the model snaps a precise mask to it. Maps directly onto promptable segmentation with point/box prompts. | SAM/SAM2 with box and point prompts, MobileSAM/EfficientSAM for lighter local inference |
| **Landscape component masking (adaptive landscape)** | could | high | Semantic segmentation of landscape scenes into categories like vegetation, water, mountains, architecture, and ground, exposed as maskable targets and used by adaptive landscape presets. | Mask2Former/SegFormer on ADE20K classes, OneFormer, MMSegmentation |
| **Content-aware Remove (non-generative heal/clone)** | must | medium | Classic spot removal plus content-aware fill that synthesizes plausible texture from surrounding pixels without a generative diffusion model. This is the dependable baseline every raw editor needs before generative fill. | LaMa inpainting, MI-GAN, OpenCV PatchMatch/Telea inpainting, MAT |
| **Generative Remove (Firefly-class fill)** | should | very-high | Diffusion-based object removal that hallucinates new content behind removed objects and offers multiple variations to pick from; Adobe runs it on Firefly servers with generative credits, and since mid-2026 also ships a smaller on-device offline generative model. Locally this means Stable Diffusion-class inpainting, which is heavy but workable on 8GB+ GPUs. | Stable Diffusion / SDXL inpainting (diffusers), FLUX.1 Fill (dev, non-commercial license caveat), LaMa as fast fallback, ComfyUI/stable-diffusion.cpp for local runtimes |
| **Object-aware removal selection** | should | medium | When the user roughly brushes part of a distraction, the tool expands the selection to the whole object before removal so fills do not leave fragments. Effectively promptable segmentation feeding the inpainting mask. | SAM2 mask expansion from scribble prompts, GroundingDINO for text-prompted variants |
| **Lens Blur (AI depth-based bokeh)** | could | high | Estimates a monocular depth map, lets the user set or refocus the in-focus range (including subject-tracked focus), and renders synthetic defocus with selectable bokeh styles (circle, ring, cat-eye) plus a boost slider for highlight rendering. Depth models run fine locally; convincing physically-plausible bokeh rendering is the hard half. | Depth Anything V2, MiDaS/ZoeDepth, Marigold (diffusion depth), BokehMe rendering research code, custom scatter/gather defocus shader |
| **Reflection Removal** | wont | very-high | Separates glass reflections from the transmitted scene in photos shot through windows, exposing the reflection layer with adjustable strength for non-destructive correction; Adobe processes this in the cloud. Open equivalents are research-grade and fragile on real photos. | DSRNet, Dereflection research models (single-image reflection separation literature), location-aware SIRR codebases |
| **Adaptive Presets** | should | medium | Presets that embed an AI mask reference (sky, subject, people body parts, landscape components) so a single click applies adjustments only to the relevant region of each specific photo — e.g. whiten teeth, enhance sky, smooth skin. Cheap to build once the masking model family exists; the work is preset format design. | Own masking stack (SAM/BiRefNet/face parsing), XMP-like preset schema with mask-type references |
| **Adaptive Profiles (AI tone mapping)** | could | high | Content-aware color and B&W profiles that analyze each image and apply an image-specific tone map rather than a fixed LUT, giving a balanced HDR-like starting point. Comparable to learned image-adaptive enhancement operators. | Image-Adaptive-3DLUT, NeurOp, exposure-correction nets trained on MIT-Adobe FiveK |
| **Auto settings (Sensei-trained)** | must | medium | One click sets exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, saturation, and vibrance using a network trained on large volumes of professionally edited photos, personalized to the image content. A regression model over image features predicting slider values reproduces this well. | MIT-Adobe FiveK and PPR10K datasets for training, CLIP/DINOv2 features + slider-regression head, Image-Adaptive-3DLUT, ONNX Runtime |
| **Enhance: Super Resolution** | should | medium | Doubles linear resolution (4x pixel count) of raw or rendered files using a super-resolution network, producing an enhanced DNG; marketed for large prints and heavy crops. Open SR models are mature and fast on consumer GPUs. | Real-ESRGAN, SwinIR, HAT, 4x-compact community models (chaiNNer/spandrel ecosystem), ONNX Runtime |
| **Enhance: Raw Details (ML demosaic)** | could | high | Replaces conventional demosaicing with a CNN trained on difficult patches to resolve finer detail and suppress false color and zipper artifacts on Bayer and X-Trans mosaics, keeping the original resolution. Open pretrained raw-demosaic weights are scarce, and X-Trans support is a real differentiator/cost. | Demosaicnet (Gharbi et al.), joint demosaic+denoise research code, LibRaw/RawSpeed for decode, own training pipeline on paired raw data |
| **Assisted Culling** | could | high | Scores an entire shoot for sharpness/focus and eyes-open state, recognizes faces so users can inspect per-person quality metrics on each frame, and filters a large take down to keepers; graduated from early access in June 2026. A composite of face detection, eye-state classification, focus measurement, and burst grouping. | RetinaFace/SCRFD + MediaPipe eye blendshapes for eyes-closed detection, Laplacian/frequency focus metrics, MUSIQ/NIMA quality scoring, embedding-based near-duplicate grouping (OpenCLIP + HDBSCAN) |
| **Best Photos (album highlights)** | could | medium | Cloud feature that auto-selects the strongest images from an album using aesthetic and technical quality signals plus face criteria, with a slider controlling how many it keeps. Same building blocks as culling, packaged as a one-shot ranking. | NIMA/MUSIQ aesthetic models, face quality scoring, duplicate clustering |
| **Face recognition and People view clustering** | should | high | Detects faces across the library, clusters them into unnamed people, lets users name/merge/hide clusters, and continuously assigns new imports; runs on-device in Classic and server-side in cloud Lightroom. Fully replicable locally with open face embeddings and clustering, though model licenses need care. | InsightFace (SCRFD detect + ArcFace embed; check license), AdaFace, dlib/FaceNet as permissive alternatives, HDBSCAN/DBSCAN clustering, sqlite-vec/FAISS for embedding storage |
| **AI semantic search (search without keywords)** | should | medium | Cloud Lightroom lets users type natural-language queries like 'red car on beach' and finds matches via automatic content analysis, no manual keywording required. Locally this is CLIP-style joint embeddings over the library plus a vector index — proven viable on consumer hardware by Immich and PhotoPrism. | OpenCLIP, SigLIP/SigLIP2, FAISS or sqlite-vec, Immich/PhotoPrism as reference architectures, ONNX Runtime |
| **Auto-tagging (Sensei content tags)** | should | medium | Automatically assigns category and object tags to uploaded photos to power search facets and filtering, invisible to the user until they search. Open tagging models produce comparable label sets on-device. | RAM/RAM++ (Recognize Anything), OpenCLIP zero-shot classification against a tag vocabulary, YOLO-World for object tags |
| **Quick Actions (AI-suggested one-tap edits)** | could | high | Mobile/cloud panel that analyzes the photo and surfaces contextual one-tap fixes — blemish removal (now with a manual Refine view), subject/background adjustments, teeth whitening — based on what it detects in the image. It is orchestration on top of the masking and removal stacks. | Face parsing + skin-blemish detection heuristics, LaMa for blemish fill, own masking stack for suggestions |
| **Content Credentials (C2PA provenance)** | could | medium | Attaches signed provenance metadata on export (currently JPEG) recording creator identity, linked social accounts, and edit/AI-usage history, either embedded in the file or published to a public credentials cloud; in early access across the Lightroom ecosystem. Adobe's own C2PA implementation is open source, making this unusually easy to clone. | c2pa-rs / c2patool (Content Authenticity Initiative open source), C2PA specification, local signing key management |
| **AI-edit metadata tracking and filters** | could | low | Catalog flags recording which photos were touched by AI tools (Denoise, generative edits) with library filters to find them, plus a manage-AI-edits view in cloud Lightroom. Mostly catalog schema work. | Catalog DB fields + XMP history entries |
| **On-device ML runtime and acceleration layer** | must | medium | Cross-cutting infrastructure Lightroom relies on: models execute via GPU or NPU per platform (Neural Engine on Apple Silicon, DirectML-class acceleration on Windows) with graceful CPU fallback and background batch processing. For a clone this is the single most leveraged engineering investment since every AI feature sits on it. | ONNX Runtime with CoreML/DirectML/CUDA/ROCm execution providers, ncnn, MLX (Apple), model download/versioning manager |
| **Third-party AI model integration** | could | medium | June 2026 added an integrated Topaz sharpening/denoise pathway inside Lightroom, pointing at a plugin surface where external AI models process photos within the workflow. An open clone could generalize this into a documented model-plugin API. | ONNX model plugin spec, spandrel (loads community SR/restoration checkpoints), chaiNNer ecosystem conventions |
| **Preset recommendations (ML-ranked community presets)** | wont | medium | Cloud Lightroom suggests community presets ranked by visual similarity between the user's photo and photos the presets were built for. An embedding-similarity ranking problem over a preset library. | OpenCLIP image embeddings, FAISS similarity search over preset exemplar embeddings |
| **Automatic red-eye and pet-eye correction** | could | low | Detects and recolors flash-induced red pupils in humans and the green/white variant in animals with one click. Solved with classical CV plus a small detector; low effort, expected by users. | OpenCV Haar/DNN eye detection, simple chroma-based pupil recolor |
| **Auto geometry / Upright perspective correction** | should | medium | Analyzes line structure in the image to automatically level horizons and correct converging verticals, with full/level/vertical/guided modes. Classical vision rather than deep learning, but users perceive it as 'AI'. | LSD/ELSD line segment detectors, OpenCV homography estimation, lensfun for lens profiles |
